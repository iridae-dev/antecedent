# Bayesian IV and sharp-RD staged references

The Bayesian IV Study route uses a joint linear Gaussian structural model with a fixed unit loading of the treatment disturbance in the outcome and known unit stage variances. Its low-level 300-replicate exact-law check covered 285/300 nominal 95% intervals, and the public staged check covered 282/300. A separate free-loading control-function prototype covered 1.000 of 300 intervals and remains unlicensed.

The licensed RD route uses sharp assignment and a known 2.5 cutoff jump under a local-linear Gaussian outcome law. Its posterior conditions on the explicit rectangular bandwidth and plug-in outcome variance. The low-level suite checks prior and bandwidth sensitivity and refuses departures from sharp assignment; staged 95% interval coverage was 0.957 over 300 declared-law repetitions (three Monte Carlo standard errors 0.038).
