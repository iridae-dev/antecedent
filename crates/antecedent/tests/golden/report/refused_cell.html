Blockers and refusals

IdScientific impossibilityReason
support.refusedyesCheap and full validation of a change attribution on a supplied or accepted Cpdag, Pag, Admg, TemporalAdmg, TemporalCpdag or TemporalPag is refused. The validation would refit the vector of total change and component contributions on random row subsets and on bootstrap resamples drawn within each of the two populations, and report one joint chi-square statistic against the original. The total change is a difference of two population means and the allocation a Shapley function of mechanism swaps, so a within-population subset or resample leaves the replicate mean at the original whatever heterogeneity is planted: the same derivation holds for every member of a class and every atom of a posterior, which are each attributed by the DAG or noise-block route whose replicates are such subsets and resamples. A verdict that cannot tell a sound attribution from an unsound one is not evidence, so the cell refuses instead of reporting one. Next attempt: a planted-violation design for the allocation (a mechanism that differs between the halves of one population), checked against an exact reference.
