This table distinguishes a checked identification result from an executable
provider and from an interval license. The support registry and
`parity/transport_stages.toml` remain authoritative for their existing cells.

The bounded catalog-aware decision checks the complete single-source theorem
premises before reporting a z-specific line-11 obstruction. It retains the
previous stronger-family classical obstruction. Missing evidence, unsupported
input, and exhausted search never become nonidentification proofs.

The scoped point route has a separate versioned artifact whose consumer rechecks
the proof, catalog, laws, provider snapshots, and result. Multi-source
restricted-experiment identification and Bayesian execution remain refused.
