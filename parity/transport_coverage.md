# Transport coverage for the 2.1 development branch

This table distinguishes a checked identification result from an executable
provider and from an interval license. The support registry and
`parity/transport_stages.toml` remain authoritative for their existing cells.

| Query and graph | Evidence premise | Identification | Execution | Uncertainty |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Classical single-source transport on the already licensed selection ADMG contract | Complete source experimental family and catalog-bound target law | Licensed within the classical theorem contract | Exact and finite empirical providers | Only separately recorded bootstrap cells |
| Registered four-variable surrogate graph: W→Z→X→Y, W→Y, W↔Y, Z↔Y, Z↔X | One named source and one target; a bound joint `do(Z=z)` margin covering W, X, Y at the cited value | Checked `Σ_w P_s(y\|w,x,do(z)) P_s(w\|do(z))`; the complete experimental family is not needed for this positive formula | Rust and Python exact or empirical preparation, point estimation, refresh, and independent versioned artifact consumption; numerical SCM truth checked | `no_interval_reported` |
| Direct source exchange for a joint intervention on up to two controllable treatment variables | Concrete joint intervention values, joint outcome margin, and checked separation of population selection from outcomes after intervention | Checked direct source exchange | Exact point execution and proof/artifact replay; joint values retained | `no_interval_reported` |
| Other finite discrete z-transport diagrams up to six observed and two controllable variables | Catalog may contain joint or marginal intervention laws | Recursive reduction can produce a checked positive formula when it finds one; a failed search remains `NotCertified` without a complete negative certificate | Point execution only after every factor binds to an actual joint law; an extended surrogate graph has a checked formula and binding fixture | No interval reported |
| Registered surrogate graph with separate experimental margins or a missing selected joint regime | Required joint law is unavailable | Formula may be certified, binding refuses with the missing factor | Refused until actual matching evidence arrives | Refused |
| Selected confounded outcome with an independently checked classical s-hedge | Complete actual source experimental family and target observational joint law | A checked obstruction under the stronger all-experiments family implies obstruction for restricted experiments; portable snapshot carries the witness | Refused | Refused |
| Multi-source limited experiments or mixed-source rule search | Multiple source catalogs | Refused; reserved for a later slice | Refused | Refused |
| Fixed graph outcome-kernel contamination, including the compatible registered surrogate formula | Checked baseline proof, shared outcome kernel across arms, and complete categorical support | Incompatible formula shapes refuse | Exact response range, witnesses, tipping fraction, and independent sensitivity-artifact replay | Assumption range, not a sampling interval |
| Candidate evidence planning and arrival for the registered surrogate formula | Frozen failed catalog, feasible declared study and cost, matching actual provider snapshot and joint regime | A candidate ranks only after isolated hypothetical binding; arrival re-identifies against available evidence | Portable failure snapshot and replayable proposal; no effect estimate until evidence arrives | Cost rank is not a probability of success |

The bounded route includes recursive positive reductions and a checked
stronger-family obstruction, but does not yet implement a complete z-specific
negative decision within the declared size cap. Missing evidence is distinct
from a structural obstruction; a failed search is not a nonidentification proof.

The scoped point route has a separate versioned artifact whose consumer rechecks
the proof, catalog, laws, provider snapshots, and result. The bounded general
TRz theorem, a checked negative z-obstruction, and multi-source execution
remain closed.
