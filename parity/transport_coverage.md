# Transport coverage for the 2.1 development branch

This table distinguishes a checked identification result from an executable
provider and from an interval license. The support registry and
`parity/transport_stages.toml` remain authoritative for their existing cells.

| Query and graph | Evidence premise | Identification | Execution | Uncertainty |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Classical single-source transport on the already licensed selection ADMG contract | Complete source experimental family and catalog-bound target law | Licensed within the classical theorem contract | Exact and finite empirical providers | Only separately recorded bootstrap cells |
| Registered four-variable surrogate graph: W→Z→X→Y, W→Y, W↔Y, Z↔Y, Z↔X | One named source, one target, complete joint source `do(Z=z)` laws at the cited values, with W, X, Y measured together | Checked `Σ_w P_s(y\|w,x,do(z)) P_s(w\|do(z))` | Standalone Rust prepared route and Python identify → prepare_exact → estimate/refresh; exact and empirical point evaluation tested against independent SCM truth | No interval reported |
| Other finite discrete z-transport diagrams up to six observed and two controllable variables | Catalog may contain joint or marginal intervention laws | Refused as `NotCertified`: the general bounded theorem procedure is not implemented | Refused | Refused |
| Registered surrogate graph with separate experimental margins or a missing joint regime | Required joint law is unavailable | Formula may be certified, binding refuses with the missing factor | Refused | Refused |
| Multi-source limited experiments or mixed-source rule search | Multiple source catalogs | Refused; reserved for a later slice | Refused | Refused |
| Fixed graph outcome-kernel contamination | Checked source outcome factor and compatible target parent laws, complete categorical support | Baseline transport proof must verify | Exact assumption range and tipping point | Assumption range, not a sampling interval |
| Candidate evidence planning | Finite declared feasible catalog and cost | A candidate ranks only after hypothetical identification and factor binding succeed | Replayable proposal; no effect estimate | No probability of success |

The surrogate route implements one checked graph-specific reduction. It does
not implement the complete single-source z-transportability decision procedure
within the declared size cap. Missing evidence is distinct from a structural
obstruction; a failed search is not a nonidentification proof.

The current route has no durable zTR artifact format, independent consumer, or
artifact replay. Those lifecycle stages remain closed.
