A positive catalog decision binds the joints the formula cites. The complete
source experiment family and target observational joint are required only for a
z-specific line-11 obstruction, which is sound and incomplete within twelve
observed and four controllable variables. The decision retains the previous
stronger-family classical obstruction. Missing evidence, unsupported input, and
exhausted search never become nonidentification proofs.

The scoped point route has a separate versioned artifact whose consumer rechecks
the proof, catalog, laws, provider snapshots, and result. Multi-source
restricted-experiment identification and Bayesian execution remain refused.
