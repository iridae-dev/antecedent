## What changed after the initial audit

The initial audit found a real cache-level defect: an atom failing identification
at one horizon was discarded from all horizons. The cache now retains each
horizon certificate, including failures, and each output slice computes its own
eligible atoms and unidentified mass. A deliberately failed certificate regression
pins this property directly. Bounded three-variable, lag-one graph enumeration
did not produce a natural differing-eligibility example; that search is not a
theorem about larger graphs.

The other three gaps required additional implementation: two documented mean
mechanism overlays; validation that refits a complete Sustained window rather
than a last-step surrogate; and an observed-data Bayesian temporal model rather
than treating nuisance-corrected outcomes as complete Gaussian observations.
The latter uses Gaussian Markov-blanket conditionals, including future child
equations, with initial-history priors explicitly recorded. Selection-indicator
trajectories or censoring-bound trajectories must be independent of the latent
outcome trajectory conditional on fully observed conditioning trajectories.
Censoring event indicators themselves depend deterministically on the outcome
and bounds; their inequality constraints remain in the likelihood.
