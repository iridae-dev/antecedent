# Antecedent 1.0.0

The contract freeze. 1.0.0 is the version bump of the 0.9.1 matrix: no code
changes, no cells added or removed, no new query kinds, no new identification
theories. What changes is the promise. From this release, the public API, the
licensed support matrix, artifact format 0.4, and the scientific refusals do
not move except by a later major version.
Workspace and Python package versions are **1.0.0**.

1.0 ships now because every public sentence is true under the gates that run.
0.9.0 made support closed-world; 0.9.1 made the overclaiming rows keep their
own promises — `full` runs the predictive checks its name claims, observation
cannot silently ride a complete-data pin, temporal standard errors are one
contract, and the licensed Bayesian temporal and panel intervals have coverage
evidence on known DGPs rather than a generic footnote.

## What is frozen

- **The Python root namespace and stage modules.** `import antecedent` stays
  at its 49 frozen names; each stage module keeps its separately frozen
  `__all__`. See [api_naming.md](../api_naming.md).
- **The support matrix.** 77 licensed cells of 1406 meaningful coordinates,
  every other cell a typed impossibility or a refusal with a reason on file.
  Compatible 1.x minors may license new cells under the existing query kinds,
  graph classes, and identification theories; they may not remove or weaken
  any licensed cell, and refusals carry their existing typed errors.
- **Artifact format 0.4.** Durable artifacts, migration, and cross-language
  round trips are stable for the 1.x series. See
  [artifacts.md](../artifacts.md).
- **The refusal boundary.** Fail-closed stays the posture. Transport and
  interference remain stage APIs and are never folded into `analyze`.
  Observation assumptions are never inferred from columns. Priors do not
  upgrade identification.

## What 1.0 is not

It is not CausalFusion completed. General sID with negative certificates,
graph-posterior mixtures over `a ↦ E[Y | do(A=a)]`, multi-source
meta-transport, cyclic and equilibrium models, and observational interference
remain post-1.0 science. ML CATE remains a documented handoff, not an
absorption. The [roadmap](https://github.com/iridae-dev/antecedent/blob/main/ROADMAP.md)
records the 1.x sequence and the permanent non-goals.

## Explicit refusals

The [support matrix](../support-matrix.md) is authoritative; omission from the
licensed block below means refusal unless the coordinate is one of the matrix's
typed n/a rules. 1.0.0 changes no refusal — the boundary is exactly 0.9.1's:

- `Counterfactual`, static `MediationEffect`, `AverageDerivative`,
  `PointDerivative`, `Elasticity`, `SemiElasticity`, `DirectionalDerivative`,
  and `ResponseJacobian` have no licensed analysis cell. They remain importable
  so callers get a typed refusal rather than an absent API.
- `Cpdag` has no licensed analysis cell. Incomplete `TemporalCpdag` and
  `TemporalPag` analysis is likewise refused; completing one to a
  `TemporalDag` is a different coordinate.
- Bayesian response curves/intervention responses, response mixtures over
  graph posteriors, Frequentist graph-posterior analysis, and non-DAG posterior
  atoms (`Admg`, `Cpdag`, or `Pag`) are refused. `TemporalDag` DBN-posterior
  pulse/single-step-sustained validation-`none` cells are the documented
  temporal exception.
- Accepted or graph-posterior path-specific and interventional-distribution
  analysis is refused; their only licensed cells are explicit-DAG,
  Frequentist, validation-`none`.
- Bayesian temporal mediation and temporal-mediation cheap/full validation are
  refused. Multi-step sustained windows remain estimator-refused; the licensed
  sustained form is single-step.

A licensed cell still means the recorded staged behavior and evidence contract
hold. It does not verify the cell's causal assumptions from the observed data,
and it does not establish universal frequentist or Bayesian calibration.
