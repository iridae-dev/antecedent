# Antecedent 2.1.0 release notes

## The 2.1 release in one view

2.1 keeps the identify → estimate → inspect → refresh → export → consume lifecycle of 2.0 and widens the questions that lifecycle can carry. Randomized experiments, held-out policy value, longitudinal regimes, difference-in-differences and related designs, and survival contrasts prepare and analyze as retained studies. Interval claims for those studies are rows of a graphless support matrix, separate from the geometric matrix. A row is a support license for that claim. It is not a coverage record: calibration for this cut has not been run, and `result.calibration` is still the calibration status. Network exposure keeps its geometric Bernoulli cell and adds graphless rows for cluster-randomized, saturation, and observational contrasts. On the structural side, restricted experiments identify a target effect from the experiments a source can actually run, a fixed-DAG natural direct effect shares one abduced world across both treatments, and Bayesian estimation covers attribution, trial transport, network interference, and class-posterior intervention response. Every geometric-matrix route executes from a checked operation sealed at preparation. A result still carries its identification, support, uncertainty, assumptions, provenance, and limits.

## Design studies on the same contract

The new families live on stage modules, not the package root: `experiment`, `factorial`, `policy`, `regimes`, `quasi`, and `survival`. Prepare freezes the design with the study. Refresh reuses that frozen design and can take outcome-only data; it does not re-read design columns from the new table. On the experiment, policy, regime, and interference queries, row-aligned inputs may be named by data column, and prepare resolves those names.

Randomized designs cover Bernoulli, complete, stratified, cluster, fixed-cell 2×2 factorial, and independent multi-arm assignment, plus switchback sequences, Wald complier effects, one-sided treatment on the treated, fixed-coefficient CUPED, and ANCOVA on pre-assignment covariates. Policy evaluation scores a precomputed binary or multi-action policy on held-out randomized subjects, including finite-class regret, uplift bins, and a kernel-smoothed group-to-dose policy under a declared known density. Longitudinal regimes evaluate a prespecified static or history-adaptive rule by g-formula, sequential doubly robust scores, or an additive marginal structural model. The caller supplies the conditional rewards or stabilized weights those scores condition on. Quasi-experimental designs cover two-period and repeated-cross-section difference-in-differences, staggered adoption with a never-treated event study, synthetic control and synthetic difference-in-differences (including a donor-trained ridge correction and an exact assignment test of a stated constant effect), fuzzy regression discontinuity, and regression kink. Survival contrasts cover individually randomized RMST and fixed-horizon survival, delayed entry, known censoring weights, and competing risks. Simultaneous curve bands are their own graphless rows.

The [graphless support matrix](../graphless-support-matrix.md) is the support license for these claims. A design can return a point when no row matches. A matching row licenses that claim under its stated support; it does not calibrate the interval. A pointwise row does not confer a simultaneous band. A caller-supplied nuisance, density, or propensity is not independently verified.

## Restricted experiments

2.0 transport assumes a source can experiment on the variables the classical theorems name. 2.1 adds z-transport for a target effect identified from one source's experiments on a bounded controllable set: finite discrete diagrams of up to twelve observed variables and four controllables. A positive decision is a checked formula bound only to the joints that formula cites, including a registered surrogate route. A line-11 obstruction is a structural fact about the selection diagram and the declared controllable set: an empty catalog and the full experiment family reach the same terminal, and replay rechecks it. Two sources are searched separately. Complementary factors combine only under two registered factorizations — disconnected outcome components, or one connected graph whose intervened outcomes separate into two groups given the treatments — and a request that would invent a joint over both sources' interventions is refused by name.

The same route reports an assumption range for outcome-kernel contamination, with witnesses and a tipping fraction. That range is not a sampling interval. A planner can test hypothetical catalog additions, rank checked sufficient studies by a caller-supplied cost, and replay the proposal when the evidence actually arrives. The point artifact carries the proof, catalog binding, laws, and program; an independent consumer rechecks them and recomputes the point. Empirical cited tables can publish a nominal percentile bootstrap or a posterior equal-tail interval. Both carry `estimator_grid_not_measured`. No coverage record attests them, and this cut does not treat them as calibrated. See the [transport coverage matrix](../../parity/transport_coverage.md).

## Nested counterfactuals and a wider Bayesian scope

The fixed three-node natural direct effect replays both treatment worlds from one abduced exogenous table. Under a non-separable outcome the frozen mediator carries that unit's disturbance into the direct effect, so the point is the shared-abduction value rather than a disturbance-free plug-in. The effect is point-only, and worlds outside that mediation contract refuse. See the [counterfactual coverage matrix](../../parity/counterfactual_coverage.md).

Bayesian estimation, still model-scoped, now includes anomaly and change attribution on a shared row law, trial-to-target transport, finite-network interference, and CPDAG and PAG intervention response under a graph posterior. Conditional effects take multiple declared modifiers. Response curves can report a simultaneous band from the same joint posterior draws. Finite-discrete structural transport accepts an empirical-support Bayesian bootstrap and a declared-state-space Dirichlet posterior. Sharp regression discontinuity has a bandwidth-conditional local-linear posterior, and a linear instrumental-variables route uses a joint Gaussian structural model. A quadratic g-computation basis and a cross-fitted robust average effect are available as procedures whose intervals do not carry a calibrated coverage claim.

## Providers, without rebuilding Antecedent

`extensibility.CausalProviderSpec` is the contract for an analysis Antecedent does not implement. A Python provider is registered explicitly, or loaded from a separately installed entry point, and `analyze` returns a `ProviderAnalysisResult` that keeps the provider's output, uncertainty semantics, and trust boundary. It does not recast plugin output as a native point or interval. Caller-supplied verification fixtures can mark an exact request verified; unseen requests stay externally attested. An EconML handoff still encodes through the existing artifact path. External packages need no Rust or Python rebuild, and a verified provider does not receive a native support-matrix license.

## What is deliberately not claimed in 2.1

The [support matrix](../support-matrix.md) is the license for graph, evidence, inference, and validation coordinates. The graphless matrix is the license for design-family claims. Neither matrix is a calibration pass. An implemented query is not permission to combine every design, horizon, nuisance, and uncertainty statement.

In particular: synthetic control and synthetic difference-in-differences report a point, and their exact assignment p-values test a stated sharp null. They have no effect-interval row. The direct factorial utility reports cell means and covariance-free variance bounds and has no graphless row; the retained factorial randomization is a separate row. Sequential inverse-probability regime values and augmented panel difference-in-differences stay point-only. Caller-fitted censoring or propensity routes stay point-only where no row names them. Coupled capacity or budget constraints keep a policy value point-only. Longer regime horizons do not inherit the two- and three-period rows. Three or more ANCOVA covariates stay point-only. z-transport is sound and incomplete inside its variable bounds. A support license is not a coverage claim. `calibrated` still requires a coverage record that matches the execution and attests the executing code, and that pass has not been run for this cut.

## Explicit refusals

Unlisted geometric coordinates and unsupported priors or target populations still refuse. A graphless design that misses its row has no support license for that claim. A point may still be returned. See the [support matrix](../support-matrix.md#refusal-reasons) and the [graphless support matrix](../graphless-support-matrix.md).
