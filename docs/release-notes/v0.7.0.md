# Antecedent 0.7.0 (draft)

Draft notes for the temporal-response cut. Package version remains **0.6.1**
until release acceptance. Do not tag or publish from this draft.

## Theme

Time as a response, not a contrast: dose × horizon surfaces and temporal
intervention paths on `TemporalDag`, reusing temporal-backdoor identification
(ADR 0021). Artifact format advances to **0.4**.

## Newly licensed cells

- `ResponseCurve` × `TemporalDag` × `explicit` / `accepted` × `Frequentist` × `none`
- `InterventionResponse` × `TemporalDag` × `explicit` / `accepted` × `Frequentist` × `none`
- `PulseEffect` × `TemporalDag` × `explicit` / `accepted` × `Frequentist` × `none`
- `SustainedEffect` × `TemporalDag` × `explicit` / `accepted` × `Frequentist` × `none`
  (single-step window only; multi-step remains estimator-refused)

`InterventionResponse` on `TemporalDag` licenses Soft(`constant` /
`additive_shift`) and a single-step `Sequence` only; a multi-step or nested
`Sequence` fails closed with a stable error rather than silently collapsing
to its last step. Temporal response pointwise bands now compute the
delta-method standard error of the g-computed level from the full
homoskedastic OLS coefficient covariance (propagating intercept and
adjustment-covariate uncertainty), replacing the previous treatment-coefficient-only
approximation that produced a zero-width band at dose zero.

Evidence: `conformance/response/temporal_dose_horizon`, including Pulse /
Sustained numerical agreement with the dose × horizon surface via shared
`TemporalLinearAdjustment` machinery (not derivation by slicing the surface).
