# Antecedent 1.5.0

**Unreleased — in release preparation.** Local review and tests are complete for
the scoped implementation; this is not a publication or clean-tree CI sign-off.
See the [release checklist](../development.md#releases),
[Python walkthrough](../local-distributional-joint.md), and

## What's new

- Prepared binary AIPW and discrete joint-cell AIPW scores support retargeting to
  a declared covariate-weighted population without refitting nuisances.
- Exceedance/CDF grids publish per-arm estimates, raw-score covariance, bands,
  and tail support. Licensed AIPW paths also estimate quantile treatment effects.
- Cell-saturated AIPW captures non-additive joint effects. CoDetermined tier
  backgrounds certify single and joint adjustment sets on their known closure.
- Prepared batches share folds and covariate bases when adjustment sets agree,
  rebuilding them for each new table. Nuisance fitting and some initial design
  allocation remain per query.
- Static class-aware estimates retain shared-row covariance and unidentified
  mass. Candidate-selection provenance records the surfaced claim family and
  screen/estimate rows.

## Correctness fixes

General-ID scalar responses now return the requested intervention mean: the
front-door fixture gives 0.3 at Set(0) and 0.6 at Set(1), rather than returning
their 0.3 contrast as a level. Conditional CDFs transform outcomes exactly once.
Prepared estimates and refreshes use the new table's covariates. Prepared QTEs
and retargeting retain their quantile functional. Unsupported tails do not
publish zero-width inference, and unavailable bands survive CBOR round trips.

The `functional_validation` PathSpecificEffect cell was frozen in 1.2
(`7c5b91a8`) at `path_effect` 1.0 and shipped that number through 1.4. On that
table `P(y|t=1)=0.8` and `P(y|t=0)=0.5`; g-formula through `m` recovers the same
`0.8−0.5`. A difference of binary means cannot be 1.0. 1.5 returns 0.3.
Comparing numbers against 1.2–1.4: that difference is an intended correction.

## Explicit refusals

- **Retargeting:** standardization within one population, not transport. Weights
  must depend only on the certified adjustment set, with parents declared in
  `depends_on`. Unknown-tier plans have no single score table and refuse retargeting.
- **CDFs:** projected monotone CDF values are distinct from raw-score bands and
  covariance. AIPW score-table bands use exceedance coordinates; conditional-grid
  bands use CDF coordinates. The walkthrough shows the conversion. Support flags
  govern whether bands can be reported, even if unsupported bounds are finite.
  Multi-threshold results have no scalar ATE.
- **Quantiles:** explicit Frequentist AIPW AverageEffect on DAG or CoDetermined
  closures, initially AllObserved, including prepared retargeting. Uncertainty
  conditions on a finite piecewise-linear CDF grid and excludes interpolation
  bias and grid selection. Projection-altered crossings, inadequate density,
  and unsupported tails refuse. Binary ConditionalEffect with one modifier also
  supports Frequentist DAG/CPDAG/PAG quantiles of modifier-standardized arm CDFs;
  mixtures require aligned influences and zero unidentified mass. Explicit
  cell-AIPW estimates the requested joint-cell quantile level on DAG/CoDetermined.
  Quantiles require `refute="none"`; mean refuters and E-values do not apply.
  Other estimator families refuse instead of substituting a mean estimate.
  First-click CustomDistribution grids and quantiles refuse; use AllObserved
  prepare plus retarget for the licensed weighted versions.
- **Joint cells:** discrete Set interventions on DAG or CoDetermined closures.
  Unknown-tier joint cells and point CDE via `continuous_cell` remain refused.
  Scalar DAG cheap/full validation uses cell contrasts for cell-AIPW; plugin
  response levels use overlap and sampling-stability checks as licensed.
- **Class-aware response ID:** generalized adjustment first, then existing ID on
  each MAG-as-ADMG completion. This is complete-then-ID, not PAG-native ID/IDC.
  General-ID estimation supports AllObserved scalar intervention means and
  exceedances; identify-only curve witnesses do not license curve estimation.
  Bare ADMG InterventionResponse remains refused. Static atom IFs are mixed only
  when every identified atom supplies an aligned IF and unidentified mass is zero.
- **Deferred:** temporal/DBN multi-atom Frequentist uncertainty stays with 1.9;
  the Frequentist DBN-posterior combiner stays with 1.7. PN/PS/PNS, policy
  learning, and transport through `analyze` are not licensed. EconML is a handoff.

AIPW inference requires positivity and suitable nuisance convergence rates;
cross-fitting alone does not establish them. The two-MAG coverage experiment
uses the same adjustment functional in both atoms, not arbitrary PAG mixtures.

## Performance evidence

Joint tier closure constructs a candidate in O(p) for fixed treatment count,
then materializes and checks a dense ADMG. The full operation is not linear in p.
The 667-variable facet-clique test took **0.46 seconds in an optimized build** on
the review machine. Its unoptimized regression ceiling is **16 seconds**, not
a measured optimized runtime or a universal performance guarantee. The 200-node
regression has a separate unoptimized timing budget. The named
`tiered.no_latent_to_outcome` premise is shared with single-lever identification;
its attached E-value approximation is not an identification certificate.

## Python joint-cell routing

Direct DAG `analyze(..., estimator="cell.aipw")` now routes through the prepared
cell estimator. DAG and CoDetermined paths preserve outcome functionals, including
joint quantiles, across first-click, prepared estimation, and retargeting.
