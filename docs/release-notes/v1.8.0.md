# Antecedent 1.8.0

Released 2026-09-13. Bayesian remainder of the staged handle: functional
path/distribution/ADMG, static mediation, counterfactuals, derivatives, and
ConditionalEffect graph-posterior mixtures. The
[support matrix](../support-matrix.md) is the license. Evidence:
[v1.8-evidence.md](../v1.8-evidence.md). Mathematical and engineering review:
[v1.8-review.md](../v1.8-review.md).

## What changed

- `StudyBuilder::inference` no longer forces `bayesian.gcomp` onto
  incompatible identifiers. Path, distribution, ADMG, mediation,
  counterfactual, and derivative estimators read inference mode.
- A shared functional Bayesian evaluator licenses PathSpecific,
  InterventionalDistribution, ADMG ATE, and Dag `general.id` front-door
  riders at none/cheap/full.
- Static Bayesian mediation (cheap/full) and counterfactual (`none`)
  publish four result axes. Derivatives stay on Riesz/point/GAM ids.
- ConditionalEffect × Dag × graph_posterior is licensed for Frequentist
  and Bayesian. Published moments are E[τ | identified]; unidentified
  mass is retained as a separate axis.
- Staged prior transfer for static ATE/CATE, response mappings, and mapped
  mediation mechanism hydrate; fail closed on incompatible catalogs.
  Graph-posterior composed with transfer stays 1.10.
