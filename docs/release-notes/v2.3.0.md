# Antecedent 2.3.0

Changes since Antecedent 2.2.0. The release adds reusable causal results, checked decision and study-planning handoffs, selective recalculation, and measured uncertainty for five additional method families.

## Consumer workflows

| If you need to… | Start here | What 2.3 adds |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Carry a result into a decision, inverse query or reusable artifact | [Result lifecycle](../2_3-lifecycle.md) and [decisions](../2_3-decisions.md) | Named quantities, units, support and source provenance survive supported handoffs; aligned laws enable probability, quantile and nonlinear utility |
| Rank the next study using current evidence | [Study ranking](../2_3-design-ranking.md) | Typed structural and numerical objectives, checked prior-to-signal inputs, explicit costs and independently consumed rankings |
| Update part of an analysis when inputs change | [Selective recalculation](../2_3-recalculation-capabilities.md) | Retained adjusted, doubly robust, finite response/transport, Bayesian, temporal and design state; receipts identify executed work and compatible reuse |
| Use uncertainty in Bayesian transport, nested-Markov effects, repeated histories or recovered observations | [Population, time and uncertainty](../2_3-population-time-uncertainty.md) | Method-specific measured scalar intervals with original-source replay and refusals outside the validated protocol |
| Combine external results with native analysis | [External evidence](../2_3-external-science.md) and [composition](../2_3-composition.md) | Checked finite responses, aligned laws and bounded mean callbacks retain provider identities and their declared trust |
| Compare structural or sensitivity scenarios | [Sensitivity and robustness](../2_3-sensitivity-and-robustness.md) | Per-scenario evidence, decision ranges, covariance-aware comparisons and preserved assumption boundaries |

The five new measured families are joint Bayesian transport, learned joint transport, nested-Markov Bayesian and Fisher inference, dependent temporal intervals, and sampled observation recovery. Their public adapters return `inference.MeasuredInference` only for the named scalars and validated graph, data, prior and numerical protocol. Loading an exported result independently replays its original source and verifies the applicable evidence. These method-specific licenses do not extend to arbitrary models, parameter-wide intervals or simultaneous coverage.

The original source artifacts and diagnostic summaries retain their declared statistical meaning. A mean supplies no joint law; structural ranges and sensitivity ranges supply no sampling confidence. Decisions from fitted effect models, general transported path-specific counterfactuals, discovery recalculation, adaptive policies and arbitrary callback/cross-world execution remain outside 2.3's supported workflows. See the linked guides for their exact boundaries.

## Python API changes

The new `decision.Contract.evaluate` supports checked decision inputs; `design.rank_designs` adds typed objectives including declared structural sufficiency and cost; `design.rank_structural` remains a convenience view. [The naming dictionary](../api_naming.md) lists the current stage modules and supported Rust/Python entry points. [Examples](../examples.md) use the 2.3 API.

## How to read the claims

| Term | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `point_only` | An exact or plug-in point estimate. No interval of any kind is claimed. |
| `nominal` | An interval whose coverage was never measured at its own coordinate (`estimator_grid_not_measured`). Unmeasured diagnostics grant no coverage license. |
| `calibrated` | An interval with a coverage record measured at its own coordinate; shown to readers as a *statistical interval*. A cell is described this way only once its records exist. |
| `structural_envelope` | The range of an answer over explicitly supplied structural scenarios. Not a confidence interval. |
| `assumption_range` | The range of an answer over a declared sensitivity set. Not a confidence interval. |
| `none` | No numerical claim: identification or inspection only. |
| statistical interval | The reader-facing name of a `calibrated` interval; it appears only beside a measured coverage record. |

## Explicit refusals

A result is available only at its declared scientific and support coordinate. Unidentified contracts, incompatible units, quantities, populations or regimes, insufficient support, overlapping evidence and an unavailable provider capability produce structured refusals. A mean contrast does not supply absolute potential-outcome means, a probability, a quantile or a joint law. Structural sufficiency ranking does not supply an identification probability. Recalculation requires the original retained execution state and a supported analysis family; an imported artifact alone does not restore that state.

Joint and learned joint Gaussian transport, nested-Markov Bayesian and Fisher inference, temporal intervals and sampled recovery require passing evidence for the exact method, prior, protocol, scalar and sample-size scope. Unsupported settings, stale evidence and source mismatches refuse. The measured result licenses only its named scalar endpoints; the underlying source artifact's parameter summaries and diagnostics retain their original meaning. Temporal responses support studentized intervals; sampled recovery supports BCa intervals. Checked paired effects support their separately measured studentized, percentile and basic constructions.

Direct decisions from `prediction.FittedEffectModel` and general transported nested/path-specific counterfactuals remain unsupported in 2.3.
