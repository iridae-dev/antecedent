# Antecedent 2.4.0

Changes since Antecedent 2.3.0. The release connects measurements, fitted mechanisms, external evidence, transported claims, adaptive experiments and decisions through checked composition: each handoff states what it carries, what it cannot supply, and what it refuses. Every new uncertainty route is derived and unmeasured. This page records each new cell with its inference status, the adjacent requests that are refused, and the artifact compatibility classes.

## Consumer workflows

| If you need to… | Start here | What 2.4 adds | Inference status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Correct a measured exposure or outcome, or use an external latent posterior | [Measurement transport](../guides/transported-decisions-and-measurement.md), [interface contracts](../guides/interface-contract-adapters.md) | Classical error, repeated measurements, binary misclassification, categorical confusion matrices, provider latent posteriors, one measurement-model contract | point-only |
| Compose fitted mechanisms and propagate dependent uncertainty | [Composition](../composition.md), [composed uncertainty](../guides/composed-uncertainty.md) | Acyclic joint law under intervention; linear moments, aligned nonlinear functionals, known-weight mixtures; spread and exceedance of the composition | point-only and derived-unmeasured |
| Pool, borrow from or revise evidence without double counting | [External evidence](../external-science.md), [assumption range and revalidation](../guides/assumption-range-and-revalidation.md) | Row-level overlap, minimum-variance and random-effects pooling, hierarchical borrowing, a correlation range, revalidation after a source changes | point-only; assumption range for unknown correlation |
| Transport counterfactuals, mechanisms and cohort effects | [Transport and counterfactuals](../transport-counterfactuals.md), [mechanism transport](../guides/mechanism-and-synthesis-transport.md), [discovered structure](../guides/discovered-structure-transport.md) | Nested and conditional counterfactual transport, sampled transport, transported mechanisms, class-of-structures transport, provider-sourced evidence, cohort-effect decisions | point-only, exact-finite or structural envelope |
| Decide or invert on composed, cohort or transported sources | [Inverse and decision reach](../guides/inverse-and-decision-reach.md), [decisions](../decisions.md) | Sources state before execution what they supply; their identity and transport receipt travel into the result | follows the source |
| Judge how reliable a sampled decision or ranking is | [Decision reliability](../guides/decision-reliability-uncertainty.md) | Monte Carlo error and effective sample size, ranking probabilities, selection-aware evaluation | derived-unmeasured |
| Run and analyse an adaptive experiment | [Adaptive procedures](../guides/adaptive-procedure-uncertainty.md) | Analysis contract, procedure matrix, posterior, decision-theoretic stopping, adaptive weighting, repeated measures, anytime-valid and group-sequential readings | point-only; guarantees closed |
| Assess a predictive law, or condition on an observation | [Capabilities](../capabilities.md) | Proper scores, score differences, calibration diagnostics, drift; observation-conditioned probabilities and evidence requirements | point-only |
| Re-run discovery with a changed setting | [Selective recalculation](../recalculation-capabilities.md) | Stage reuse under a changed threshold, multiplicity rule, background knowledge, orientation budget, lag window or CI test | none (reuse accounting only) |
| Call a provider you do not own | [Provider-sourced transport](../guides/provider-sourced-transport.md) | Law, posterior and nested callbacks with replay policy, portable results and re-derived identities | none (claims, not native facts) |

Bayesian counterparts of the vector, categorical, dose, mediation, latent-class, penalized and clustered score, finite-law decision, recovery and transport routes return a posterior mean as a point. Their draws are never offered as an interval.

## Inference status

Nothing new in this release is claimed as measured coverage. Cells below carry one of `point_only` (exact-finite or plug-in point), `assumption_range` (value over a declared premise range) or `none` (identification, inspection or a closed adjacent claim). Derived uncertainty is exact under its stated model and carries derived-unmeasured standing. Existing measured scalar intervals from 2.3 keep their records and scope; none of them extends to a composed, transported-new or adaptive result by inheritance, and a calibration record is never carried to a new target or composition.

## Refused adjacent requests

| Request | Reason | Remedy |
| --- | --- | --- |
| An interval, posterior or coverage statement for pooled, borrowed, composed, bridged, revalidated, transported-composed, sampled-counterfactual, cohort-mean or adaptive results | No record at that coordinate; a mean carries no joint law | Use the point or the assumption range; measure the coordinate |
| Ranking probability, optimality set or selected-value interval from a point-only producer or independently drawn candidates | Candidates must come from one joint law | Keep per-draw utilities from one joint law |
| Source-spread, Monte Carlo and selection uncertainty as one combined number | Distinct meanings are not added | Report them separately |
| A probability, quantile, nonlinear utility or joint law from a cohort mean or a point source | A mean supplies no law | Supply draws or a finite table |
| A mechanism behind a selection arrow, or a transport with no declared uncertainty | The premise is untested | Refit in the target; declare the variance and covariance |
| A cross-world value in another population, with sampled abduction, or with an interval | Mechanisms are declared for one population | Declare the population; use the point |
| A cyclic composition, or inherited calibration from an input without a matching upstream record | Calibration is never inherited | Break the cycle; re-measure |
| Fixed-sample inference on an adaptive design, posterior under value-dependent loss, coverage of an adaptive result | Paradigms are not interchangeable | Model the loss; use the adaptive-weighting or anytime-valid paradigm |
| Unknown overlap treated as disjoint, or independence declared where overlap is unknown | Overlap is decided from row-level identity | Register the sources in the evidence ledger |
| A score interval, significance level, callback-only law, or causal validity from a predictive score | Scores describe realized observations only | Use the score as a diagnostic |
| A callback supplying a posterior guarantee, native trust, identification licence or joint law | Callback results are claims | Verify the object; use a native route |

[What these routes do not do](../user-facing-limits.md) lists each package's most likely refused request and remedy. A refusal carries a registered reason code, a typed detail and a remedy.

## Known open item

Calibration of the composed, decision-reliability and adaptive-procedure uncertainty routes is measured in the release candidate. Until then these routes report derived-unmeasured standing, and no result is issued as `calibrated` without a coverage record at its own coordinate. Measured coverage that exists for 2.3 intervals is unchanged. The acceleration workstream moved to 2.5 and does not affect any claim here.

## How to read the claims

| Term | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `point_only` | An exact or plug-in point estimate. No interval of any kind is claimed. |
| `nominal` | An interval whose coverage was never measured at its own coordinate (`estimator_grid_not_measured`). Unmeasured diagnostics grant no coverage license. |
| `calibrated` | An interval with a coverage record measured at its own coordinate; shown to readers as a *statistical interval*. A cell is described this way only once its records exist. |
| `structural_envelope` | The range of an answer over explicitly supplied structural scenarios. Not a confidence interval. |
| `assumption_range` | The range of an answer over a declared sensitivity set. Not a confidence interval. |
| `none` | No numerical claim: identification or inspection only. |
| statistical interval | The reader-facing name of a `calibrated` interval; it appears only beside a measured coverage record. |
