# Antecedent 2.1.1

2.1.1 corrects 2.1.0. It licenses no new coordinate and withdraws none. The [changelog](https://github.com/iridae-dev/antecedent/blob/main/CHANGELOG.md) lists every change; this page covers what an upgrading user should check.

## Estimates that change

Synthetic difference in differences now fits its time weights to the donors' post-period means, as the method specifies; 2.1.0's weights never moved from uniform. Fuzzy regression discontinuity and regression kink count rows exactly at the cutoff on the treated side. Negative binomial g-computation standard errors are no longer scaled by √α. Bayesian IV draws are decorrelated across nearby seeds. Transported priors carry the variance of their reweighted mean and drop a source whose weights have too few effective units. Badly scaled conjugate Gaussian designs are solved after equilibration. For mediation results, `effect()` now returns the queried contrast.

## Artifacts verify what they claim

A study built without an explicit estimator now receives the same distribution replay and AIPW binding checks as one that names it; in 2.1.0 an edited artifact of that kind could still verify. Distribution scalars must match their atoms, a standard error requires its estimate, and structural masses, manifest trailing bytes, and total declared section sizes are checked on load. 2.0.0 anomaly-attribution artifacts keep their digests.

## Prepared studies and transport

Refreshing a prepared study with a different row count rebinds its retained fit, and rebound validators reach the sealed routes. Two-source z-transport no longer multiplies factors taken at different treatment levels, and duplicate evidence binds by one documented rule on every path.

## Results that are now refused

Where 2.1.0 returned a wrong answer and no small change recovers the right one, 2.1.1 refuses: kNN conditional-independence tests on two or more continuous conditioning variables, invalid or misaligned CI weights, clustered or HAC standard errors for matching, exact ratios over an unobserved stratum, and graph files a reader cannot represent faithfully. Elsewhere the correct result is now computed instead — for example, non-integer category codes are treated as labels, and a conditioning set that determines a variable gives exact independence.

## Explicit refusals

2.1.1 licenses no new coordinate and withdraws none. Unlisted geometric coordinates still refuse; see the [support matrix](../support-matrix.md#refusal-reasons) and the [graphless support matrix](../graphless-support-matrix.md).
