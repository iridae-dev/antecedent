## Method boundaries

- Bayesian non-Complete temporal curves use an observed-data Gaussian SEM
  with latent-trajectory Gibbs sampling under declared ignorable joint-trajectory
  CAR and distinct priors. `crates/antecedent/tests/temporal_observed_bayesian.rs`
  consumes all five observation pairs and a multi-step Sequence. This is a
  parametric likelihood route, not Bayesian IPCW/KM/Cox weighting.
- Sequential full validation uses actual mechanism refits for perturbation
  checks. Single-regression sensitivity formulas are marked inapplicable;
  unobserved-confounder perturbations refit the complete sequential effect.
  PPC statistics remain separate for different child mechanisms and mix only
  alternative graph atoms for the same child.
- A joint horizon mediation posterior needs a shared dynamic parameter model.
  Independently fitted horizon posteriors are reported as pointwise slices.
- Unrestricted latent-confounded PAG natural mediation needs additional
  cross-world identification theory. The linear DAG mechanism product does
  not establish it.
- Temporal graph-posterior response surfaces need per-cell categorical
  model draws and horizon-indexed structural mass. No aligned cross-model
  draw averaging is substituted.

## Review regressions

- `envelope::tests::large_location_does_not_erase_small_mixture_variance`
  checks centered empirical-mixture moments at a large location;
  `categorical_draws_do_not_alias_ordered_component_samples` checks an
  alternating component posterior that deterministic index thinning distorts.
  `invalid_weights_and_draws_are_errors` and
  `tiny_weights_preserve_mass_and_conditional_moments` cover invalid and
  extremely small weights.
- `observation_bootstrap_tests` in the facade checks failure thresholds and
  cancellation accounting. Passing these tests establishes implementation
  behavior, not nominal repeated-sampling coverage of the new bands.
