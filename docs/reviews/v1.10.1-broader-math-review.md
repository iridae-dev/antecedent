# 1.10.1 broader mathematical correctness pass

This pass follows `v1.10.1-math-rereview.md`. It changes computations and graph
operations; it adds no refusal conditions and does not run or regenerate calibration.

## Corrections

### Probit likelihood, score, and observed curvature

The old implementation derived the inverse Mills ratio by subtracting two very
large negative log values. At an adverse predictor of -1e8 and outcome 1, the
unit-weight score was about 65,659,969 instead of 100,000,000, and curvature was
clamped to zero instead of approaching one. This affects the shared Laplace/HMC
likelihood primitive.

A continued fraction now computes the inverse Mills ratio and its small excess
over the tail magnitude separately. The curvature uses their product, avoiding
both cancellations. Upper-tail log probabilities use `log1p` of the complementary
tail instead of being set to zero at predictor 8.

Tests cover reflected outcomes, fractional likelihood weight, deep tails, an
independent standard-library erfc reference at -10, and finite-difference score
and curvature checks on both sides of the continued-fraction boundary.

### Zero-weight observations

Likelihood accumulation previously evaluated zero-weight rows before multiplying
by zero. A large Gaussian residual therefore produced a NaN log posterior;
Poisson rates could overflow; extreme Bernoulli rows could incorrectly flag
separation. Both posterior-value and derivative accumulation now skip these
rows' contributions. Tests check equivalence to deleting the row across Gaussian,
Poisson, probit, and logit families, including separation diagnostics.

### Conditional identification graph surgery

IDC's rule-2 check removed directed arrows into intervention nodes but retained
bidirected edges incident to those nodes. Those edges represent latent-parent
arrows that the intervention must also remove. Leaving them in can create false
conditional dependencies. The surgery now removes those incident edges while
retaining latent confounding elsewhere. Outgoing-edge deletion is explicitly
scoped to the single conditioning variable currently considered for exchange.

This follows the intervention graph and IDC definitions in
[Shpitser and Pearl, Identification of Conditional Interventional Distributions](https://ftp.cs.ucla.edu/pub/stat_ser/r329-uai.pdf).
A collider regression and 384 comparisons covering all directed/bidirected edge
subsets in a three-node topological ordering compare the mixed-graph operation
with the equivalent explicit-latent DAG operation. The change corrects the
identification calculation, rather than introducing an unsupported-result guard.

## Broader review and validation

Source inspection also covered conditional-ID normalization, IV relevance and
exclusion checks and their parametric status, binary-IV response-type bounds,
interference exposure and variance calculations, split-chain R-hat/ESS formulas,
and existing exact discrete-SCM identification checks. No additional confirmed
corrections were made in those areas. This is a bounded review, not a proof of
correctness for every route or of statistical coverage.

All four targeted regression tests were reproduced against the previous
implementation, then the fixes were restored. An additional exhaustive small-graph
oracle test checks the graph operation more broadly.

Passing validation:

- 344 estimator, 148 identification, 117 probability, and 220 statistics unit tests.
- 36 expression, 107 graph, and 33 numerical-kernel unit tests.
- 5 Bayesian-likelihood and 10 facade-gap integration tests.
- 1,020 distinct passing tests in total; one identification test remains ignored.
- Unit tests named calibration or coverage were excluded. Calibration records,
  coverage evidence, and calibration jobs were not generated or changed.

The missing calibration record documented in the preceding review remains
unresolved. These checks do not establish calibrated interval coverage.
