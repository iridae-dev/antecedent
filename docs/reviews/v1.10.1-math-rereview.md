# 1.10.1 mathematical re-review

This review expands the earlier graph-posterior review into shared numerical
routines and estimator formulas. It examines the current working tree, including
existing uncommitted 1.10.1 changes. It is a source review with deterministic
regressions and existing unit/contract checks, not a proof of every algorithm or
an empirical calibration of coverage.

## Confirmed errors and fixes

### Relative-weight scaling changed numerical answers

Relative weights define a target through `w_i / sum(w)`. Multiplying every weight
by the same positive finite constant must leave means, covariance, Kish effective
sample size, and frozen-weight mixtures unchanged (up to rounding).

The original routines formed raw weight sums and squared weights. For example,
two graph weights of `1e308` overflowed their sum and turned the mixture of
`[1, 3]` and `[5, 7]` into `[0, 0]` instead of `[3, 5]`. Target weights scaled by
`1e-200` caused covariance underflow and effective-count failures. These errors
could also suppress support or quantile uncertainty.

The weight calculations now rescale by the largest weight before normalization,
squaring, or accumulation. The change covers weighted means, joint influence
covariance, Kish effective size, frozen mixtures, threshold support counts, and
quantile influence normalization. Invalid contributing weights/scores are
rejected rather than silently producing numerical certainty. Likelihood weights
in Bayesian fitting are **not** rescaled: their absolute scale can encode
likelihood tempering and is a different mathematical object.

Files: `crates/antecedent-estimate/src/joint_if.rs`, `scores.rs`, `quantile.rs`.

### Small nonconstant weights bypassed target-change checks

An absolute `1e-12` comparison classified weights such as `[1e-16, 2e-16, ...]`
as constant. These weights change the target just as much as `[1, 2, ...]`, but
could bypass the requirement to declare their covariate dependencies. Constant
weights now require equality of finite entries, independent of units.

File: `crates/antecedent-estimate/src/retarget.rs`.

### Column order could reverse a causal contrast

Retargeting subtracted the first score column from the second instead of locating
the columns for control arm 0 and active arm 1. A correctly labelled table in
reverse order therefore returned the negative of the requested effect.

Contrasts now use arm identifiers and a common functional: mean columns or a
single exceedance threshold. A threshold grid does not silently supply an
arbitrary first-threshold scalar contrast.

File: `crates/antecedent-estimate/src/retarget.rs`.

### Missing completion estimates could produce apparently complete intervals

Floating-point `min`/`max` ignore a NaN operand. The identified-set interval builder
could therefore accept `[finite, NaN]` point estimates, omit the missing
completion from its extrema, and still report an interval for both completions.
Both frequentist and posterior interval paths now reject non-finite completion
points and misaligned point/SD arrays.

File: `crates/antecedent-estimate/src/identified_set.rs`.

### Invalid contrast variance could become zero uncertainty

The final `max(variance, 0)` converted NaN and materially negative quadratic-form
variances into a zero standard error. Contrast summaries now reject non-finite
inputs/results and materially negative variance. Only small negative residuals
consistent with cancellation in the quadratic form are clamped to zero.

File: `crates/antecedent-estimate/src/scores.rs`.

### Bayesian validation pooled effects whose aggregation was withheld

The static Bayesian graph-posterior route withheld its scalar under
`GraphDependentAtoms`, but still pooled effect refutations and prior-sensitivity
summaries across the different estimands. It also passed the withheld (NaN)
scalar into predictive-validation reports. This differed from the Frequentist
route and from the aggregation policy governing the reported effect.

Effect refuters, predictive checks, and prior-sensitivity checks now retain
per-atom targets in this case. The ordinary same-estimand aggregation path is
unchanged.

File: `crates/antecedent/src/analysis/execute/bayesian_path.rs`.

## Other source checks

The review also inspected the following calculations without establishing another
specific defect in the portions examined:

- Wald IV ratio and influence calculation; intervention-contrast scaling and
  homoskedastic 2SLS variance.
- Front-door product-of-path coefficients and stacked cross-stage sandwich
  covariance/gradient.
- Conditional linear effects and the modifier-distribution contribution to
  influence and variance.
- First- and second-order response derivative transformations and the scaling
  of response multiplier-band inputs.
- Piecewise-linear CDF inversion and its implicit-function derivative.
- Temporal Bartlett capture, AR recursion, block resampling, and Bayesian
  mediator/outcome coefficient-product composition.
- Known-variance Gaussian conjugate precision calculations and multiple-testing
  adjustment formulas.

These checks do not establish the identification assumptions for arbitrary user
data, uniform interval coverage, or correctness of every route in the repository.

## Verification

Eight new deterministic regression tests cover the fixes. The first five were
run before their fixes and reproduced failures in the original implementation.

- 344 estimator unit tests passed.
- 114 probability unit tests passed.
- 220 statistics unit tests passed.
- Calibration/coverage-named unit tests were excluded; no calibration jobs or
  calibration records were generated or changed.

The broader facade run also exposed stale tests that assumed a pooled scalar
for graph-dependent estimands. One was reproduced with all five numerical files
restored to their pre-review versions, then those review changes were restored.
The assertions now verify both numerical atom effects, retained graph weights,
and bounds claims. The claim-tampering test now actually changes an absent scalar
instead of mapping over `None` and leaving the artifact untouched.

The calibration-record completeness check fails on the missing record
`cov.average_effect.cpdag.frequentist.analytic_se.l90.class_posterior_frequentist_ate_joint_if_nominal_90_coverage`.
It is left unresolved and excluded from the final non-calibration contract run;
no evidence record was fabricated to satisfy it.

Final facade verification passed: 130 contract tests (the record-completeness
check excluded), 13 licensed static-cell tests, 6 compiler-consistency tests,
1 retarget allocation test, and 2 envelope-refuter tests. Together with the
678 unit tests, this is 830 distinct passing checks. The five edited numerical
files pass rustfmt checks; `git diff --check` passes.
