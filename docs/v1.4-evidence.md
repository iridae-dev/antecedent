## Verification

Licensed rows require `scripts/gate_support_matrix.sh` to pass.

### Adjacent completion regressions

`joint_class_response` (Rust) and `test_joint_class_response` (Python) integrate
Bernoulli, Gaussian and categorical policies through the static Bayesian additive
Gaussian model, including both joint treatment columns. The fixture's policy mean
is 7.0. This is posterior uncertainty about a policy mean; policy-draw variability
is not substituted for posterior uncertainty. Support remains unverified for the
full stochastic policy even when its mean lies inside the observed range.

These adjacent regressions are complemented by the full release and CodeQL
gates recorded in the final verification section.

### MAG visibility correction

Singleton and conditional PAG identification now apply MAG amenability and the
proper back-door graph (Perkovic et al., 2018, Definition 6 and Theorem 7), using
the shared edge-visibility check. Invisible causal first edges cannot certify
adjustment. Side-effect edges are retained in the separation graph so collider
paths cannot disappear merely because they start with an outgoing treatment edge.

The older three-node PAG response/conditional numerical pins above are withdrawn:
those graphs have invisible causal edges. Their historical numerical outputs do
not establish valid identification. Replacement positive evidence uses an explicit
visibility witness and the known law `y = 1 + 2t`, consumed by Rust
`mag_visible_numeric` and Python `test_mag_adjustment_visibility` and
`test_pag_admg_numeric_pins`. Tests cover singleton/conditional effects, curves,
intervention responses, mixed completions, explicit/accepted structures, both
inference modes, and prepared reuse. The scalar Python matrix also covers
none/cheap/full validation. The original graphs remain negative regressions,
and their invalid numerical outputs are labelled withdrawn in the JSON fixtures.
The DAG and CPDAG interpretations are distinct and their existing
valid numerical evidence is not invalidated by this MAG correction.

### Temporal completion integration

The mixed-graph implementation now retains directed/bidirected temporal MAG
refinements, including a future circle-arrow whose only valid refinement is
bidirected. Definite future-to-past arrows remain invalid. Stationary unfolding
is audited within the finite query window; audit and history caps remain explicit.

Rust `temporal_mag_numeric` consumes `temporal_class_envelope/latent.json` across
explicit/accepted structures, Pulse/single-step Sustained, fresh/prepared execution,
and none/cheap/full validation. It checks the known lagged effect 2 and E-value
standardization against the aligned outcome rows. Temporal sensitivity diagnostics
also operate on the aligned regression rows; this is a perturbation diagnostic,
not a claim of iid temporal sampling uncertainty. The validate crate tests pass.

`completion_agreement_uses_a_shared_temporal_indexer` verifies that equivalent
completion formulas initially unfolded with different histories share coordinates
before envelope comparison. The source expression arenas remain intact.

Python `test_temporal_mag_adjustment` passes all 25 cases after rebuilding the
native extension, including the fully oriented class regression. The affected graph/identify/validate Clippy checks also pass.
Full release and CodeQL verification subsequently passed.
