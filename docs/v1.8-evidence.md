# Antecedent 1.8 evidence

This ledger names the consuming tests for the 1.8 Bayesian remainder of the
staged handle. Identification, support, uncertainty, and assumptions remain
separate axes. Estimator ids stay licensed; inference mode is applied at
execute time. `bayesian.gcomp` is not forced onto functional, GCM, or Riesz
identifiers.

## Query-aware inference

- `StudyBuilder::inference` sets `bayesian.gcomp` only for static
  `AverageEffect` (or an unset query). Path, distribution, ADMG, mediation,
  counterfactual, and derivative studies keep their identifier-native
  estimators:
  `bayesian_does_not_rewrite_functional_estimators` in
  `crates/antecedent/tests/v18_bayesian_remainder.rs`.

## Functional Bayesian evaluator

- Path-specific natural effects, interventional distributions, ADMG
  front-door ATE, and a Dag `general.id` rider share a Bayesian-bootstrap
  evaluator of the identified functional: one Rubin Bayesian-bootstrap
  Dirichlet(1, ..., 1) / Exp(1) row-weight draw over observed rows induces
  every CPT factor, marginal, and alias. This is not a conjugate
  multinomial–Dirichlet(1) update. Published distribution atoms are the
  empirical point-estimate law; the scalar posterior is E[functional(p)].
  This preserves probability identities and covariance between factors;
  unobserved joint cells retain zero mass. Diagnostic
  `functional.dirichlet`.
  `functional_bayesian_path_distribution_and_admg` and
  `distribution_bayesian_known_truth` consume
  `conformance/estimate/functional_validation`,
  `conformance/estimate/admg_frontdoor_functional`,
  `conformance/estimate/interventional_distribution`, and
  `conformance/context/path_specific_natural`.
- Cheap/full reuse the 1.2 path/distribution subset-stability suites.
- A backdoor coefficient artifact without a declared functional mapping is
  refused (no isotropic CPT fallback).

## Mediation, counterfactuals, derivatives

- Bayesian static mediation is a mechanism posterior then the natural-effect
  product. Pins NDE=1.8 / NIE=4.8 from
  `conformance/estimate/staged_static_kinds`. Cheap/full run
  `refute_static_mediation`. A shared coefficient prior assigned to both
  mechanisms is refused. A mapped EffectFunctional artifact hydrates
  ATE/Δ onto the outcome-mechanism treatment slope only (NDE coefficient
  β_{Y←T}); other mechanisms keep isotropic `prior_scale`
  (`static_mediation_mapped_prior_hydrates_mechanisms` consumes
  `conformance/bayesian/static_mediation_prior_transfer`):
  `mediation_and_counterfactual_bayesian_pins` and
  `transfer_without_mapping_fails_closed`.
- Bayesian counterfactuals (`none` only) publish a Bayesian-bootstrap
  mechanism posterior using direct weighted fits conditional on the selected
  mechanism families and empirical support, then abduction–action–prediction on the original
  units for every draw. `unit_effects` are the posterior mean of those
  per-unit ITEs; mean ITE pins 6.6 on the same SCM. Coefficient artifacts
  fail closed even with a mapping. Accepted / graph-posterior / cheap/full /
  nested stay closed.
- Bayesian derivatives (`none` only) keep Riesz / point / GAM estimator ids
  and apply Bayesian inference to those estimators: each ADE draw refits
  μ and α under Rubin Dirichlet(1, ..., 1) / Exp(1) row weights, then
  evaluates the weighted Riesz ADE (not a frozen-score reweight);
  point derivatives use direct weighted local-quadratic fits conditional on
  cross-fitted nuisances and bandwidth; GAM derivatives refit with continuous
  row weights, fixed knots and penalty. Neither adds a second multinomial
  resample. Coefficient priors require a mapping to these parameterizations
  and cannot be silently applied as linear-regression priors. Pins `conformance/response/staged_derivatives`.
  Graph-posterior derivative mixtures stay refused:
  `derivative_bayesian_pins`.

## ConditionalEffect × graph_posterior

- Frequentist and Bayesian compile/execute mix per-atom conditional
  identification. Published effect moments are E[τ | identified];
  unidentified mass (0.2 on
  `conformance/bayesian/known_truth_mixtures`) is a separate
  non-renormalized axis. Bayesian output is not a silent Frequentist
  reuse:
  `conditional_graph_posterior_retains_unidentified_mass`.

## Mathematical review regressions

- `joint_dirichlet_preserves_probability_identities_and_beta_moments` checks
  the chain rule, interventional aliases, zero empirical support, and Beta(1,3)
  posterior moments. This follows [Rubin's Bayesian bootstrap](https://people.eecs.berkeley.edu/~jordan/sail/readings/rubin.pdf).
- Weighted GAM and local-quadratic kernels are checked against repeated-row
  fits with fixed knots; weighted multinomial logits are checked against
  fractional category counts.
- `static_mediation_small_draw_requests_have_finite_uncertainty` refuses
  `n_draws` of 0 or 1 with a typed error (no silent rewrite to 2) and
  publishes exactly two draws when two are requested. Mechanism fitting
  disables per-mechanism adaptive stopping so every composed draw exists.

- `counterfactual_posterior_has_bayesian_bootstrap_variance` checks a saturated
  two-group model against the exact Dirichlet group-mean variance, detecting
  the extra uncertainty from multinomial resampling.
