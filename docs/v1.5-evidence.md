# 1.5 review and evidence ledger

The three previously open 1.5 completion gaps — ConditionalEffect
per-arm CDF bands and tail support, shared prepared-batch designs, and
genuine multi-atom PAG response coverage — are implemented and pinned
below. This ledger records consuming evidence. It does not treat a
passing scalar test as a universal coverage guarantee.

## Corrections made during review

- Joint `cell.aipw` now estimates the requested intervention cell, including
  zero levels, instead of returning a 2×2 interaction for every query. Invalid
  levels and incomplete Set intervention bundles refuse.
- Weighted named cell contrasts use the weighted summary and covariance.
  Named `interaction` on an exceedance grid is refused; request a single
  Exceedance threshold or a raw coefficient vector.
- Joint nuisance fitting uses a multinomial-logit likelihood. Empty training
  cells refuse instead of supplying zero outcome regressions and tiny invented
  propensities. Continuous-grid inputs are validated; `StudyBuilder::continuous_cell`
  snaps the extra coordinate to a declared grid (coarsened treatment, not a
  point intervention at fixed `D`).
- Target-local support checks every joint cell using original held-out
  propensities. Scores retain observed cells, outcomes, and row indexes through
  artifact round trips. Missing support evidence is not treated as success.
- Weighted covariance agrees with unweighted covariance for constant weights.
  Nonfinite scores and invalid covariance matrices refuse. Cross-fitting is no
  longer described as sufficient on its own for efficient inference.
- AIPW custom distributions require declared weight parents. Their estimate,
  influence sequence, and optional refitted bootstrap use the weighted target.
  Failures no longer silently fall back to an unrelated full-sample estimate.
- Prepared exceedance estimates use the new data's scores. Refresh commits
  retained data and scores together after successful execution. Explicit AIPW
  plans preserve configured propensity options and propagate score-build failures;
  iid score inference refuses trimming and dependent-row SE configurations.
  Other estimator plans no longer silently fit an additional AIPW estimator.
- Paths that cannot execute the requested outcome transform or full grid now
  refuse instead of returning a mean or silently reducing the grid to its first
  threshold. Frequentist `ConditionalEffect` grids (Dag/Cpdag/Pag) publish
  per-arm `F_a(c)` and 2K raw-score simultaneous bands with per-threshold
  tail support. Class-aware Cpdag/Pag grids mix per-arm IFs by frozen
  completion weight, the same rule as the mean CATE mix; a contributing atom
  without an aligned IF refuses rather than mixing covariance from a subset.
  Stored class-aware mean-CATE influence is the frozen-weight mixture, not the
  primary atom. Multi-threshold grids clear the scalar `ate` and that influence
  rather than leaving a first-threshold or mean-CATE number. `cell.aipw` joint
  responses publish per-arm `F_a(c)`. Exceedance grids cannot be reduced through
  the indicator helper to the first threshold. CustomDistribution +
  exceedance grid / quantile refuses instead of returning a mean of `Y`.
  First-click CustomDistribution of a multi-threshold grid stays refuse;
  licensed weighted grid is AllObserved prepare + retarget.
- Score artifacts retain covariance, simultaneous raw-score bands, tail event
  support, and target-local support. Bounded isotonic probabilities are disclosed
  on the estimate object (`monotone_rearranged`); the covariance and simultaneous
  bands describe the raw AIPW estimates and remain attached to their explicit
  `raw_means`. Only the scalar simultaneous interval is cleared on rearrangement;
  raw covariance is not uncertainty for the projected CDF. Multi-threshold grids do not publish a first-threshold scalar `ate`.
- Unknown tiers retain separate canonical-scenario effects and simultaneous
  intervals instead of a single averaged ATE. Two scenarios are not exhaustive
  completions or bounds on the unknown graph class. Unknown-tier prepare
  exports no score table (two canonical sets / GraphDependent / no single Z);
  one table would collapse the envelope, so `retarget` refuses. Converting
  Unknown tiers to an ADMG refuses rather than dropping within-tier
  uncertainty. CoDetermined estimates attach a VanderWeele + outcome-SD
  E-value approximation for the named no-latent-to-outcome premise
  (`tiered.evalue.vanderweele_approx`). This
  is not a tier-identification certificate; tier-closure is a fast path on a
  complete-tier ADMG. Bidirected CoDetermined used to default to
  `general.id` (AIPW refused). It now defaults to generalized adjustment +
  AIPW. Singleton-tier pins never hit this; same-tier siblings (`z↔u` or
  `t↔u`) are the real fixture.
- Static effect and batch covariance align influence rows in the original row
  universe. DAG `InterventionResponse` publishes a plugin IF and joint
  covariance; class-aware responses mix atom IFs only when every contributing
  identified atom supplies an IF aligned to original row IDs (including differing
  complete-case masks) and unidentified mass is zero. An unidentified completion
  NaNs the envelope SE rather than publishing a primary-atom interval.
  Additive GAM intervention IFs include coefficient-fitting and empirical
  covariate-average uncertainty conditional on fitted knots and the penalty. They
  exclude knot selection, smoothing bias, and policy integration error and are not
  nonparametric efficient IFs. Curve IF exports to joint covariance are scaled as
  row scores; local-polynomial diagnostic contributions retain their original scale.
- Batch results carry actual simultaneous intervals and BH/BY adjusted p-values.
  `PreparedBatch` / `BatchStudy::prepare` freeze per-query plans and one
  `SharedBatchDesign` (fold assignment; `[1 | Z…]` when certified adjustment
  sets agree). Propensity and outcome residualization stay per-query.
  `prepare_cells` covers discrete joint `InterventionResponse` on a DAG.
  `analyze()` and `PreparedAnalysis.prepare` accept `TieredBackground` and
  keep outcome functionals (same honesty class as `PreparedBatch` /
  `analyze_many`). Candidate-selection provenance is recorded on
  `StudyResult`, on `EffectEstimate`, and on the estimate
  artifact wire (`screen_id`, procedure, winner, family size, screen/estimate
  row indexes, disjointness). When `estimate_rows` is recorded, estimation uses
  that analysis mask. The winner is ranked on the reported estimate-sample
  family; `screen_rows` are provenance. Candidate-selection provenance is still
  attached when joint IF cannot be formed. An unavailable ranking has `winner_index = None`,
  not an invented winner 0. Recorded splits require nonempty, unique, in-range rows;
  overlapping splits remain explicitly marked non-disjoint.
- Validator computation failures are typed `ValidationOutcome::Failed`, distinct
  from scientific rejection and incompatibility. Scalar Dag `InterventionResponse`
  licenses Frequentist cheap/full. `cell.aipw` cheap/full apply overlap and
  E-value / the effect-refuter suite to the requested-cell minus control-cell
  contrast. Plugin g-comp cheap is overlap only; full is overlap plus
  sampling-stability of the g-comp level (bootstrap, data-subset, graph).
  Contrast-shaped refuters (placebo, dummy outcome, RCC/UCC, E-value,
  sensitivity, Riesz) are not licensed for a plugin intervention level.
  `ResponseCurve` cheap/full stay n/a. Temporal and class-aware
  `InterventionResponse` cheap/full stay n/a.
- EconML column export actually transforms outcomes and exports aligned weights.
  Extra modifiers need pre-treatment certification, and nonconstant target weights
  require declared covariate parents.

## Consuming evidence

retargeted cell agreement, new-data refresh, score/covariance/inference
artifact round trips, an 80-seed kernel-target coverage experiment, CPDAG
shared-row coverage, a two-MAG PAG response mix, a front-door MAG response identified

Dag InterventionResponse cheap/full with estimator-specific refute semantics,
continuous-cell grids, a 200-node tier-closure + estimate-attached E-value pin,
named-interaction grid refusal, cleared grid scalars (no first-threshold `ate`),
CoDetermined `{z,u}|{t}|{y}` and `{z}|{t,u}|{y}` + ExceedanceGrid (batch
Mean/Exceedance equals standalone at 1e-12), AllObserved first-click vs
`retarget(ones)` agreement, ATT residualized refusal,
`depends_on` without a directed graph, plugin-full contrast-refuter strip, and the
linear-plan retarget refusal. Frozen fixtures
`conformance/estimate/v15_retarget_kernel`,
`conformance/estimate/v15_cell_aipw_interaction`, and
`conformance/estimate/v15_exceedance_variance` are parsed by those pins.
The kernel-target experiment uses the population functional
`1 + 0.6/(1 + 0.7²)`, not a sample-dependent proxy. An 80-seed CPDAG experiment
covers the frozen average of the two observational functionals (2 and 2.5); it
is not a posterior over completion-specific causal truths. These Monte Carlo
tests are finite evidence, not a universal coverage guarantee.

The ADR 0011 harness (`retarget_alloc_count.rs` and
`retarget_hot_path_10k_by_500_is_bounded`) measures 500 distinct kernel
levers on one prepared 10k-row table.

Estimate-crate tests cover constant-weight covariance equivalence, malformed
covariance rejection, weighted threshold interactions, and missing training
cells. Python `test_v15.py` and `test_econml_handoff.py` exercise public
wrappers, including `PreparedBatch.shared_design`, ConditionalEffect
`score_inference` bands / tail flags, and typed candidate-selection payloads
with screen/estimate row indexes.

## Final-review regressions

The zero-shift plugin identity now checks both the sample mean and its sampling
SE. A 40-seed noisy constant-outcome-model experiment checks Set-policy interval
coverage (at least 33/40); it previously produced zero SE. A curve regression
compares exported local-WLS contributions with joint covariance, including the
finite-sample correction. A row-alignment test mixes disjoint original row sets.
The 200-node SCM now obeys CoDetermined within-tier assumptions, independently
checks m-separation in the intervention backdoor graph, and checks the known
1.8 effect against its interval. These are finite tests, not universal coverage.

Conditional CDF values are projected into [0, 1] and made monotone per arm.
Joint covariance and simultaneous bands are the 2K raw per-arm CDF
coordinates. Rearranged `exceedance_cdf` values are not mixed with those
intervals. Binary 0/1 ConditionalEffect arm IFs are cross-fitted AIPW
(nonparametric EIF under back-door, positivity, and nuisance rates).
Non-binary treatment levels keep the linear plugin IF.

Quantile treatment effects invert the AIPW CDF on a disclosed grid.

## Mathematical scope

AIPW influence inference requires positivity, suitable nuisance limits and rates,
and the stated sampling assumptions. Cross-fitting controls empirical dependence
but does not correct arbitrary joint misspecification. See
[Chernozhukov et al., Double/debiased machine learning](https://arxiv.org/abs/1608.00060).
Graph certification must be valid for the stated graph class and conditioning
set; two selected scenarios do not prove a class-wide bound. See
[Perković et al., generalized adjustment](https://jmlr.org/papers/v18/16-319.html).

The fitted-basis response sandwich separates coefficient uncertainty from
smoothing choices; see [mgcv covariance documentation](https://www.stat.ethz.ch/R-manual/R-devel/library/mgcv/html/vcov.gam.html).
