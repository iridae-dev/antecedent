# Graphless design-family support matrix

This table is separate from the graph/structure support axes in
[the geometric matrix](support-matrix.md). A query is licensed only when
its exact family, design, method, inference claim, and observed
assignment-unit counts match a row. All other combinations are refused.
That refusal means no matrix license; off-axis point results may still
execute. The route must validate its stated design and assumptions.

**binary_aipw limits:** Fixed binary policy and reference on 300 independent evaluation subjects under known 0.5 Bernoulli assignment, outcome nuisance predictions trained on caller-declared disjoint subjects, at least 10 policy and reference matches, uncoupled recommendations, positive paired score variance, and both pointwise 95% intervals. Cross-fitted nuisance ownership and learned policies remain outside this row.

**binary_aipw_uplift limits:** Fixed binary AIPW policy with disjoint nuisance training and frozen ranked uplift bins with separate declared ranking training IDs, known 0.5 Bernoulli assignment, at least 300 independent subjects and 50 observed subjects per arm in every bin, and all scalar and bin pointwise 95% intervals published. No cross-fitted or simultaneous guarantee.

**binary_ipw limits:** Fixed binary policy and reference on independent held-out subjects with known 0.5 Bernoulli assignment, at least 120 evaluation rows and 10 realized policy and reference matches, uncoupled recommendations, positive paired score variance, and both policy-value and incremental-value pointwise 95% intervals. No learned-policy inference or simultaneous claim.

**binary_ipw_uplift limits:** Fixed binary IPW policy plus frozen ranked uplift bins, known 0.5 Bernoulli assignment, declared ranking training IDs disjoint from evaluation IDs, at least 300 independent subjects and 50 observed subjects per arm in every bin, and all scalar and bin pointwise 95% intervals published. No simultaneous top-k band or ranking-model guarantee.

**multi_action_ipw limits:** Fixed multi-action policy and reference on 300 independent randomized subjects with known action probabilities at least 0.2, 10 realized policy and reference matches, uncoupled capacities and budgets, positive paired score variance, and both pointwise 95% value intervals. No action-specific CATE claim in this row.

**multi_action_ipw_cate limits:** Fixed multi-action policy plus pre-treatment baseline groups declared before outcomes, known action probabilities at least 0.2, at least 300 independent subjects and 50 observed subjects in each compared arm per group, uncoupled constraints, and all scalar and group/action pointwise 95% intervals published. No fitted CATE model or simultaneous group coverage.

**bernoulli limits:** Independent Bernoulli binary ITT with one or two pre-assignment ANCOVA covariates, a common declared probability from 0.2 through 0.8, 400 rows, 30 observed rows per arm, positive HC0 variance, and a published pointwise 95% interval. Six 2,000-allocation fixtures cover one and two covariates at probabilities 0.2, 0.5, and 0.8; three or more covariates remain point-only.

**bernoulli limits:** Independent Bernoulli binary ITT adjusted by one pre-assignment covariate with a coefficient fixed outside these outcomes, known probabilities from 0.2 through 0.8 on every row, 400 rows, 30 observed assignments per arm, positive independent-unit score variance, and a published pointwise 95% interval. Four 2,000-allocation fixtures cover both probability boundaries, the midpoint, and varying probabilities.

**bernoulli limits:** Independent Bernoulli binary ITT with known probabilities at least 0.2 in both arms on every row, 400 rows, 30 realized assignments per arm, and a published positive-variance pointwise 95% score interval. Adjustments and CACE/LATE do not inherit this row.

**cluster limits:** Known complete cluster assignment, independent clusters, binary unit-weighted ITT, at least 30 clusters per arm, and an actually published interval (positive realized variance). Conservative cluster-total variance and a pointwise 95% normal interval; no observational network exposure or other assignment design is licensed by this row.

**complete limits:** Known complete assignment, independent units, binary ITT, at least 30 units per arm, and an actually published interval (positive realized variance). Conservative Neyman variance and a pointwise 95% normal interval; no simultaneous interval, CACE, adjustment, or other assignment design is licensed by this row.

**factorial_2x2 limits:** Known fixed-cell 2×2 randomization with at least 30 units in every cell and published positive-variance intervals for both main effects and the interaction. Each 95% interval is pointwise; joint or simultaneous coverage is not licensed.

**multi_arm limits:** Independent known-probability assignment to at least three actions, 400 rows, at least 30 realized assignments to every action, probabilities at least 0.2 for every action on every row, and a published interval for every action-versus-reference contrast. The intervals are separate pointwise 95% claims; simultaneous coverage is not licensed.

**stratified limits:** Known independent complete assignment within at least four blocks, at least 15 units in every block arm and 60 per overall arm, and a published positive-variance pointwise 95% interval. This row licenses the block-weighted ITT only.
