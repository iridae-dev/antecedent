This table is separate from the graph/structure support axes in
[the geometric matrix](support-matrix.md). A query is licensed only when
its exact family, design, method, inference claim, and observed
support thresholds match a row. All other combinations are refused.
That refusal means no matrix license; off-axis point results may still
execute. The route must validate its stated design and assumptions.

**bernoulli limits:** Wald CACE/LATE from independent Bernoulli encouragement and observed treatment receipt, with known assignment probabilities at least 0.2 in both arms on every row, 400 rows, 30 realized assignments per arm, a finite positive receipt first stage, positive independent-unit influence-function variance, and a published pointwise 95% normal interval. Exclusion and monotonicity are declared, untestable identifying assumptions. A 2,000-replication known-truth fixture at 1,200 rows and a 0.5 complier share confirms coverage. Weak first stages, sparse or low-probability arms, and adjusted or ITT contrasts do not inherit this row.

**bernoulli_one_sided limits:** Treatment-on-treated among recipients under declared one-sided noncompliance: independent Bernoulli encouragement with no control-arm receipt, so every recipient is a complier and the Wald ratio identifies their mean effect. Requires known assignment probabilities at least 0.2 in both arms on every row, 400 rows, 30 realized assignments per arm, a finite positive receipt first stage, positive independent-unit influence-function variance, and a published pointwise 95% normal interval. Observed control-arm receipt is refused at query validation. Exclusion is a declared, untestable assumption. A 2,000-replication known-truth fixture at 1,200 rows confirms coverage. Two-sided noncompliance, weak first stages, and sparse arms do not inherit this row.

**fixed_group_kernel limits:** Fixed pre-outcome group-to-dose policy and reference with known correct observed-dose density, independent subjects, conditional exchangeability, at least 600 evaluation rows and 300 per group, 80 local rows, effective sample size at least 50, maximum normalized local weight 0.05, minimum density 0.2, positive paired score variance, and all three pointwise 95% intervals. Values are for the stated kernel-smoothed interventions, not exact-dose potential outcomes or simultaneous coverage.

**panel_2x2 limits:** Balanced two-period panel DiD with stable treatment, one pre and post outcome per subject, clusters nested within treatment arms, at least 30 independent clusters per arm, positive CR1 score variation, and a published pointwise 95% normal interval. Parallel untreated trends and no anticipation are declared, not tested by two periods. No staggered or augmented DiD inherits this row.

**repeated_cross_section_2x2 limits:** Repeated cross-section 2×2 DiD with unique subjects, clusters nested within treatment groups, at least 30 distinct independent clusters in every group-by-period cell, positive CR1 score variation, and a published pointwise 95% normal interval. Parallel untreated trends and no anticipation are declared. No panel, staggered, or augmented DiD inherits this row.

**staggered_event_study limits:** Balanced staggered-adoption event study against never-treated controls (cohort zero): each subject observed in every period, adoption cohort and cluster stable within subject, clusters nested within the adoption cohort and the never-treated controls. Licenses the separate pointwise 95% normal cluster interval for each post-adoption event-time contrast (event time at least zero), including the representative group-time ATT, only when the compared adoption cohort and the never-treated controls each have at least 24 independent clusters, at least 48 clusters total, positive CR1 score variation, and a published interval. One 2,000-replication known-truth coverage check re-randomizes cluster shocks at 24 clusters per group. Pre-adoption leads and the omitted event time -1 stay descriptive and point-only, as does the joint pre-trend statistic; cohort-specific parallel untreated trends and no anticipation are declared, not tested; each interval is pointwise and never a simultaneous event-study band, and under-supported cohorts, the single-target group-time design, and augmented or two-period DiD do not inherit this row.

**cluster_randomization_total limits:** Complete cluster randomization on a fixed partial-interference network with within-cluster-only edges and one cluster per unit, the (0,0)-to-(1,1) total-effect contrast under NeighborFraction exposure, at least eight independently randomized clusters in each assignment arm, positive between-cluster Neyman variation, and a published pointwise 95% Welch cluster interval. One 2,000-replication known-truth coverage check re-randomizes the cluster allocation at eight clusters per arm. The interval is pointwise for the total effect only; no direct/spillover decomposition, simultaneous band, or Bernoulli/observational design inherits this row, and partial interference and the exposure mapping are declared, not tested.

**observational_known_exposure limits:** Observational network-exposure contrast with caller-supplied KNOWN, fixed marginal exposure probabilities and an explicit, caller-declared network-exchangeability (no-unmeasured-network-confounding) assumption that the library cannot verify. Requires a fixed network with within-cluster-only edges, at least 30 independent partial-interference clusters, at least eight clusters exposed at each requested level, positive between-cluster CR1 variation, and a published pointwise 95% cluster-t interval. One 2,000-replication known-truth coverage check re-randomizes per-unit exposure with the supplied probabilities. Externally fitted probabilities carry unknown fitting uncertainty and stay point-only with a descriptive CR1 variance; the network-confounding assumption is a declared, documented limitation, never a licensed claim, and no simultaneous band inherits this row.

**two_stage_saturation limits:** Exact two-stage saturation: clusters completely randomized to declared low/high within-cluster treatment probabilities, then independent Bernoulli assignment, on a fixed network with within-cluster-only edges. Licenses a single declared direct, spillover, or total exposure contrast with at least 24 independent clusters in each of the two saturation arms, at least eight exposed clusters at each of the two requested exposure levels, positive between-cluster Welch variation, and a published pointwise 95% cluster interval. One 2,000-replication known-truth coverage check re-randomizes both stages at 24+24 clusters. Each interval is pointwise for its one contrast; no simultaneous curve or joint decomposition, and partial interference and the exposure mapping are declared, not tested.

**fuzzy_jump limits:** Fuzzy regression discontinuity as a local outcome-jump over treatment-jump ratio at a prespecified cutoff and fixed triangular-kernel bandwidth. Licenses the pointwise 95% normal ratio interval only when the estimator's own weak-first-stage screen passes (the local treatment jump is separated from zero by its HC0 95% interval), both sides retain at least five local observations for the cubic bias-correction pilot, the delta-method ratio variance is finite and positive, and the interval publishes. Local continuity of untreated potential outcomes, no precise running-variable manipulation, exclusion, and local monotonicity are declared, untestable identifying assumptions. One 2,000-replication known-truth coverage check at bandwidth 0.5 over 1,200 running-variable points confirms nominal coverage. The interval is pointwise for the local complier ratio only; weak first stages, undersupported windows, bandwidth or manipulation-robustness selection, and the regression-kink slope contrast do not inherit this row.

**regression_kink limits:** Regression kink as a local outcome-slope-change over treatment-slope-change ratio at a prespecified cutoff and fixed triangular-kernel bandwidth. Licenses the pointwise 95% normal ratio interval only when the estimator's own weak-first-stage screen passes (the local first-stage slope change is separated from zero by its HC0 95% interval), both sides retain at least six local observations for the quartic slope bias-correction pilot, the delta-method ratio variance is finite and positive, and the interval publishes. Continuity of untreated potential-outcome and treatment derivatives at the cutoff, no precise manipulation, exclusion, and local monotonicity are declared, untestable identifying assumptions.
